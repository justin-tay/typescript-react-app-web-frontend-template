## Risks

<!-- arc42-generated -->

| #   | Risk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Priority | Impact                                                                           | Probability | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | The production CSP contract is not enforced here. The built `index.html` carries a placeholder; if the host does not replace it per response and send a matching header, scripts do not run, or (if the host weakens the policy) the protection is lost. | High     | App fails to load, or loses script-injection protection                          | Medium      | Document and test the contract at deployment; add a check or sample server config. The expected headers are recorded in the [HTTP headers mapping](08-crosscutting-concepts/02-security-and-authentication/headers.md). |
| 2   | `SESSION_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` is a copy of the backend's timeout and nothing keeps them equal.                                                                                                                                                               | Medium   | The warning appears too early or too late; people are signed out without warning | Medium      | Have the backend expose its timeout, or add a deployment check.                                                                                                                                                         |
| 3   | Backend contract assumptions are untested: the `/api` prefix, `urn:problem:*` problem types, paging shape, filter names.                                                                                                                                 | Medium   | A backend change breaks pages in ways the stubbed tests cannot see               | Medium      | Contract tests, or end-to-end tests against the backend template.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 4   | No end-to-end tests and no CI configuration in the repository.                                                                                                                                                                                           | Medium   | Regressions in layout, focus and real sign-in flows are found by hand            | High        | Add CI running `build`, `lint`, `format:check` and `test`; consider Playwright against a mock server.                                                                                                                   |
| 5   | Background requests count as session activity. Anyone adding polling or a query library with refetch-on-focus would silently stop the idle timeout from working.                                                                                         | Medium   | Sessions never idle out while a tab is open                                      | Low         | Documented in section 8; add a way to mark a request as background if polling is needed.                                                                                                                                |
| 6   | The footer links in `FOOTER_LINKS` are placeholders (`#`), including "Report Vulnerability", "Privacy Statement" and "Terms of Use". A deployment that forgets to replace them has no working route for reporting a vulnerability.                       | Low      | A researcher has nowhere to report a problem                                     | Medium      | An adopter decision; a test or start-up check that fails while a required link is still `#`.                                                                                                                            |
